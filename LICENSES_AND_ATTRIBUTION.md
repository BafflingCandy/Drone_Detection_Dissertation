## FlyNetSim

This project adapts FlyNetSim, “An Open Source Synchronized UAV Network Simulator based on ns-3 and ArduPilot,” by Sabur Baidya, Zoheb Shaikh, and Marco Levorato.

- Upstream repository: <https://github.com/saburhb/FlyNetSim>
- Paper: <https://doi.org/10.1145/3242102.3242118>

The copied launcher and ns-3 scenario support files retain their upstream provenance. Project-specific changes are described in the root README and source comments.

## ns-3 and ArduPilot

FlyNetSim depends on ns-3 and ArduPilot. Their complete distributions and compiled products are deliberately excluded from this curated corpus. Install them from their official/upstream sources and comply with their respective licences.

- ns-3: <https://www.nsnam.org/>
- ArduPilot: <https://ardupilot.org/dev/>

No claim of authorship is made over unmodified third-party code.
