Scope note: the 100-seed tables define reproducible route geometry. The corpus separately contains five complete standard and five tiered-threshold ns-3 RF runs. “Tiered” means that the inner secure-wall sensors use a more sensitive −70 dBm threshold while the outer-perimeter sensors retain the configured experiment threshold; it does not mean that extra sensors were added. The route suite is broad scenario coverage, not every physically possible trajectory.

Terminology: fence24 is the internal label for 24 sensors around the modelled prison's outer fence and secure wall. The hybrid layout retains 16 directional RSSI/AoA sensors and changes 8 positions to omnidirectional RSSI anchors. “Hybrid Kalman” means that layout followed by a motion-predicting Kalman filter that smooths noisy position measurements.
